On Interaction Design

❤

Empathy First

Every interaction starts with understanding who we're designing for. I immerse myself in the user's world — their frustrations, habits, and aspirations — before sketching a single screen.

✨

Micro-Moments Matter

The space between a tap and a response, the weight of a transition, the timing of feedback — these invisible moments define how a product feels. I obsess over the details that most people never consciously notice.

🎨

Bridge Art & Function

Great interaction design lives at the intersection of beauty and usability. I believe aesthetics aren't decorative — they build trust, guide attention, and create emotional resonance.

🔬

Prototype to Prove

I don't wait for perfection. I prototype early, test often, and let real user behavior shape the final design. The best ideas come from iteration, not speculation.
